Creamy Chicken Lasagna

Description

This chicken lasagna is made with tender chicken breasts, red sauce, and lots of mozzarella cheese for a fantastic flavor combination. Everyone who tastes it raves!

This easy lasagna uses jarred spaghetti sauce for effortless layering and rich tomato flavor, while cream cheese replaces ricotta to create a rich, creamy texture. As Allrecipes member ADLUV notes, it is "a nice change from regular lasagna.

Ingredients

  • 6 uncooked lasagna noodles
